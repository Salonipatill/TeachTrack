# Campus Life page

The public `/campus-life/` page is linked from the shared homepage/campus header and footer. Its campus descriptions were checked against the official SAIT pages on 21 October 2026 and paraphrased for TeachTrack. The page links to the corresponding official facility pages.

## Information sources

- Institute overview, address and contact details: https://www.aurogroup.ac/technology/
- Common Ground, auditorium and Wi-Fi: https://www.aurogroup.ac/technology/campus-life-with-full-facilities.php
- Central library: https://www.aurogroup.ac/technology/library.php
- Sports: https://www.aurogroup.ac/technology/sports-facilities.php
- Transport: https://www.aurogroup.ac/technology/transport-facilities.php
